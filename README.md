## 📖 Overview

**Folio** is an open platform designed for thinkers, creators, storytellers, and lifelong learners. It provides a clean, distraction-free environment where individuals can publish their thoughts, share deep technical or domain knowledge, express curiosity, and broadcast compelling stories to a global audience.

Whether you are writing a quick reflective piece, a detailed tutorial, or an ongoing narrative, Folio empowers you to amplify your voice and connect with a community driven by ideas and discovery.

## ✨ Key Features (Planned & In Development)

- **Distraction-Free Editor:** A rich-text / Markdown publishing experience tailored for deep focus and effortless content creation.

- **Knowledge & Story Hub:** Categorized streams and curated feeds to help readers discover articles spanning technology, science, personal growth, culture, and creative writing.

- **Community Engagement:** Interactive discussions, responses, and social sharing features that allow writers and readers to connect deeply.

- **Customizable Profiles:** Showcase your body of work, track reader engagement, and build a personal brand around your ideas.

- **Audience Analytics:** Insights into reader engagement, reach, and story performance over time.
